Wilder Screen Keys turns the arrow keys and spacebar into a smooth, precise mouse replacement — built for people who feel pain when they reach for a mouse.

What it does

You toggle the app on with Caps Lock. From that moment, arrow keys move the cursor and the spacebar clicks. Hold Ctrl to pause it for normal shortcuts (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+S). Toggle off and your keyboard returns to normal.

Who it’s for

  • People living with arthritis, RSI, or chronic hand and wrist pain.
  • Anyone with limited hand mobility who finds mouse use exhausting.
  • Keyboard-first users — developers, writers, gamers — who want to keep their hands on the home row.
