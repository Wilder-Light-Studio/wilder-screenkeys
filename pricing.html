Frequently asked

Is the Gift Edition really free?

Yes. It is the same software with the same license terms. The only difference is that no payment is required.

Can I share my license key?

The license is yours personally — install it on all your own machines. It is not transferable to other people. If a family member or colleague needs it, they should get their own copy or request the Gift Edition.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes — see the refund policy.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards, plus Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal where available. Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy; we never see or store your card details.

Will there be a subscription?

No. The Personal Edition is a one-time purchase, and that is the only paid model we plan to offer.
